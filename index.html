01 / FEATURED PROJECT
Language→Catalogue→Action
Autoverse AI · LLM workflow platform

From a garage request
to a grounded answer.

An automotive parts platform that interprets natural-language requests, resolves vehicle fitment, retrieves catalogue evidence, and connects conversations to stock, quotations, and dispatch workflows.

Python / FastAPIQdrant hybrid retrievalGemini / GroqOllamaMySQLStreaming responses
  1. 01 / Understand

    Decode the request

    Extract vehicle and part details from text; support uploaded documents and images.

  2. 02 / Resolve

    Find the right context

    Normalize workshop vocabulary and clarify vehicle variants, part names, and fitment.

  3. 03 / Retrieve

    Search the catalogue

    Combine hybrid retrieval with variant-scoped lookup, ranking, and deduplication.

  4. 04 / Validate

    Check against evidence

    Validate agent-selected names against real candidates; ask when ambiguity remains.

  5. 05 / Connect

    Move the workflow on

    Use catalogue and stock tools, stream results, and support quotations and requests.

Retrieval & resolution

  • Qdrant dense vectors and BM25-style sparse search fused with reciprocal rank fusion, alongside exact/fuzzy part aliases and vehicle-specific lookups.
  • A bounded agentic resolver rejects names outside the catalogue candidate list, retries invalid output, and falls back to clarification.
  • Deterministic confidence scores explain the evidence behind part recommendations.

Models & conversation

  • Configurable Gemini, Groq, and Ollama model paths with fallbacks; agentic follow-ups call catalogue, stock, brand, and kit tools.
  • Persistent conversation and vehicle context preserve follow-ups such as brand preferences and fitment choices.
  • Server-Sent Events deliver batched search results and stage progress as work completes.

Application workflows

  • PDF, spreadsheet, document, and image ingestion supports text extraction and OCR.
  • PDF quotation generation and dispatch-request endpoints connect parts selection with business processes.
  • Role-based permissions, organization-aware catalogue/cache handling, and usage tracking support multiple organizations.

Evaluation & reliability

  • Provider-comparison tooling measures extraction latency and agreement with a Gemini baseline.
  • Regression coverage targets vocabulary, vehicle/session state, stream lifecycle, and organization scoping.
  • Caching, batched processing, and parallel catalogue work reduce repeated processing.
